## 5. Contact by email

If you email us, we process your details (sender, content) only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). Our mailbox is hosted by netcup (see section 4) as our processor. We delete requests once they are dealt with and no retention duty applies.

## 6. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). Write to hello@plundergames.de. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg.

## 7. Changes

If the website or the law changes, we will update this policy.
